Master the traditions and terminology

"Love" Means Zero

In tennis, "Love" represents a score of zero. Derived from the French word "l'oeuf" (the egg), symbolizing the shape of a zero.

Deuce Control

A 40-40 score is a "Deuce." A player must then win two consecutive points (Advantage and then Game) to secure the game.

Quiet, Please

Tennis is a game of extreme focus. Silence during rallies is not just a rule, it's a centuries-old mark of respect for the players.

The Handshake

Win or lose, every professional match ends at the net with a handshake. It's the ultimate symbol of sportsmanship in the Golden Era.
