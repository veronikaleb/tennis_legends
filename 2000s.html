Aerodynamic Precision

In the 2000s, rackets became precision instruments. Technologies like Babolat's AeroModular and Wilson's [K]Factor focused on swing speed and stability. This enabled the legendary consistency of Federer and the devastating topspin of Nadal on the grass.

The Rise of the Titans

The new millennium ushered in the "Golden Era" of men’s tennis. Roger Federer elevated the game to an art form, only to be challenged by the relentless physical force of Rafael Nadal. Meanwhile, Serena and Venus Williams brought unprecedented power and athleticism to the WTA, forever changing the standards of professional fitness and baseline aggression.
