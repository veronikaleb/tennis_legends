The Bold
Nineties

The decade of Agassi's neon style, the dominance of the power baseline game, and the rise of the iconic "Woodies" in doubles.

The Widebody Era

The 90s were defined by the "Widebody" revolution. Manufacturers like Head and Wilson began using Titanium and Hyper Carbon, making rackets lighter yet significantly more powerful. This allowed players like Agassi to hit winners from anywhere on the hard court.

The Grunge & Glamour Decade

The 1990s transformed tennis into a global pop-culture phenomenon. Andre Agassi brought denim shorts and neon colors to the court, while Pete Sampras perfected the art of the "Pistol Pete" serve. It was a decade of intense rivalry and the arrival of teenage sensations who proved that age was just a number in the face of raw talent.
