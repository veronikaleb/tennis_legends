Eighties

The era of high-tech graphite, neon highlights, and the transition from classic touch to explosive baseline power and athleticism.

The Graphite Revolution

As the 80s dawned, wood gave way to graphite and fiberglass. These new materials allowed for larger head sizes and much stiffer frames, enabling players to hit with unprecedented topspin and velocity that changed the game's physics forever.

Birth of the Modern Game

The 1980s saw tennis transform into a high-stakes global spectacle. It was defined by the legendary rivalry between Navratilova and Evert, the arrival of teenage prodigies like Boris Becker and Steffi Graf, and Ivan Lendl's blueprint for the modern, fitness-focused baseline game.
