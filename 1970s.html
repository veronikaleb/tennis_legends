The Golden
Seventies

A decade of revolution where tennis transitioned into the Open Era, defined by wooden rackets, white attire, and the rise of global icons.

The Wooden Rackets

Before the era of composites, the 70s belonged to wood. Rackets were crafted from layers of ash, maple, and okoume. They featured small 65-square-inch heads, offering a tiny "sweet spot" that demanded perfect timing and technique.

Standard Weight

380g - 410g

Head Size

~65 sq. in.

The Era of Change

The 1970s marked the true beginning of professional tennis as we know it. This was the decade of the "Tennis Boom," characterized by the first massive sponsorship deals, the establishment of the ATP and WTA rankings, and a surge in television popularity. It was a time when players became rockstars, bringing charisma and fierce rivalries to the court.
